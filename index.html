🏙️ Living district A fictionalized Lagos-inspired city foundation with streets, traffic, landmarks, pedestrians, and places to build a life.
💼 Everyday ambition The prototype is being rebuilt around jobs, income, rent, housing, relationships, and the choices that shape your reputation.
🗣️ Social consequences Gossip, disputes, secrets, romance, betrayal, and neighbourhood pressure will make the city react to what you do.
🤝 Multiplayer direction The target is a shared city where other players, NPC routines, and city events continue beyond your own story.
🧭 Build in progress This preview still contains legacy systems while the first Mainland life loop replaces them one outcome at a time.
🃏 Crime pays Blackjack, slots, roulette AND five-card poker at the Lucky 7, a lottery kiosk, a daily bank heist, a roaming armored cash truck, bounty contracts, turf raids — and REX the dog, who fetches cash, barks at cops and bites on command (Z).
🚀 Jetpack Buy it near spawn ($5000), press J and fly — plus a BASE-jump ring trial off the Spire and 10 golden sky hoops to thread.
🥊 Night life Bare-knuckle fight club on the waterfront after 21:00 (fists and webs only), ambulance rescues, harbor vehicle exports — and strange lights over the Spire past midnight.
👑 Become the LEGEND Press L for the 16-item completion board — finish everything and the city crowns you: a golden crown, $50,000, fireworks. 100% is the true ending.
🏙️ Living city Rob stores, seize gang turf, casino, dealership, wardrobe suits, garage, 20 hidden packages, day/night + weather.
📈 Grow XP levels unlock double-jump, web-dash & slow-mo aim · 12 achievements.
🧍 Pick a slinger WEAVER (balanced), BOLT (fast), or BRICK (tough) — each plays differently.
🗼 Landmarks Swing off the 150m Spire, the stadium, central park & the waterfront on a bigger 10×10 city.
🤖 Bosses A rival web-slinger AND a 500-armour war-mech — plus a monster truck at the dealership.
📷 Mobile Camera selfie booth, screenshot sharing, haptics — installable as an app.
🌊 The harbor Open water past the east edge — speedboats, a jet-ski, swimming, and piers to dive off.
🏁 Free-roam races Two street circuits, two harbor boat races and a rooftop swing trial — gold/silver/bronze medals and saved best times.
🏟️ The arena Wave survival at the stadium: gangs, SWAT and drones, cash and rep per wave.
💼 Empire Buy the casino, stadium and Spire for passive income — then defend them from raids or lose a day's take · reputation tiers · rotating daily challenges · garage tuning: respray, engine, armor, nitro.
🕸️ Hold W while swinging to build speed, release at the front of the arc to fly.
💰 Stylish moves bank cash the moment you land.
🟡 Walk into the yellow beam to start a mission — the type rotates each time.
🚓 Stars bring drones (2), SWAT (4), a tank (5). The Stealth suit cools heat faster.
📦 20 packages hide on rooftops — you'll only find them by swinging up high.
📱 On your phone: open the menu → Add to Home Screen to install it.
